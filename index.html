About Chye Kee

Founded with a passion for traditional craftsmanship, Chye Kee Beancurd Manufacture Co. has been dedicated to producing premium-quality beancurd products for generations. What began as a humble family business has grown into a trusted name in the industry, renowned for our commitment to authentic taste, superior texture, and time-honoured techniques.

At Chye Kee, every sheet of beancurd is made from carefully selected soybeans using traditional methods that preserve the natural goodness, delicate flavour, and rich aroma of fresh beancurd. Our manufacturing process combines artisanal craftsmanship with stringent quality standards, ensuring that every product meets the expectations of chefs, wholesalers, retailers, and customers who value authenticity.

Over the years, we have remained true to our roots while continually refining our craft. Our fresh beancurd sheets are known for their silky texture, excellent durability, and versatility in a wide variety of culinary creations. Whether used in traditional Asian dishes, modern fusion cuisine, or healthy plant-based meals, our products continue to be trusted by kitchens across the region.

Every beancurd sheet is freshly handmade and delivered while still fresh and moist, resulting in a soft, tender texture that sets it apart from dried beancurd. Unlike dried beancurd, which can be bland and rubbery, our fresh beancurd offers a delicate flavour and a naturally supple texture that enhances every dish.
